# Audit Testing --- Unattended Active Session Management

## AUD-004.1 --- Unattended Endpoint Inspection

**Objective:** Determine whether active workstations are left unlocked
and unattended.

**Procedure** 1. Select office locations and remote-work samples. 2.
Conduct unannounced observations or equivalent remote compliance checks.
3. Record whether the endpoint is active, unlocked, and unattended. 4.
Document exceptions without collecting unnecessary personal information.
5. Verify remediation and repeat testing where required.

**Evidence** - Physical floor-walk exception sheets - Approved endpoint
telemetry - Remediation tickets

**Frequency:** Monthly\
**Target:** 0 active workstations found unlocked and unattended.

## AUD-004.2 --- Timeout Configuration Validation

**Objective:** Confirm that the maximum idle-timeout policy is
technically enforced.

**Procedure** 1. Obtain current GPO/MDM configuration exports. 2.
Compare settings against the approved 15-minute standard. 3. Sample
endpoint compliance telemetry. 4. Investigate configuration drift. 5.
Verify corrective action.

**Evidence** - GPO exports - MDM configuration profiles - Endpoint
compliance dashboard - Exception approvals

**Frequency:** Quarterly\
**Target:** 100% of active corporate endpoints comply.

## AUD-004.3 --- Security Awareness Validation

**Objective:** Verify completion of workstation-security training.

**Procedure** 1. Obtain the active workforce population. 2. Compare it
to LMS completion records. 3. Identify overdue users. 4. Review
escalation evidence. 5. Confirm completion or approved exception.

**Evidence** - LMS completion reports - Training
certificates/transcripts - Escalation records

**Frequency:** Annually\
**Target:** 100% completion.

## Audit Conclusion Criteria

A control may be considered operating effectively when design
requirements are documented, evidence is complete and reliable,
exceptions are tracked, and remediation is timely.
