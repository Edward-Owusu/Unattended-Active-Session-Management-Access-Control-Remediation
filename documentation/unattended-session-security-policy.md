# Corporate Policy Template --- Unattended Active Session Management

**Document Reference:** PL-SEC-006\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Owner:** Information Security / GRC

## 1.0 Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for securing active system sessions
and protecting organizational endpoints from unauthorized access when
users leave their workstations unattended.

## 2.0 Scope

This policy applies to corporate laptops, desktops, engineering
workstations, remote-work endpoints, and facility terminals managed or
owned by the organization.

## 3.0 Mandatory Controls

### 3.1 Automated Idle-Timeout Standard

All managed endpoints must enforce an automatic screen lock after a
maximum of 15 minutes of user inactivity, unless a documented and
approved exception exists.

### 3.2 Manual Lock Prior to Departure

Users must manually lock their device before leaving the immediate
workspace for lunch, breaks, meetings, or other activities.

### 3.3 Endpoint Compliance Monitoring

Endpoint-management platforms should report compliance with the approved
idle-timeout configuration and identify configuration drift.

### 3.4 Physical Security Checks

Security or designated control owners may perform periodic unannounced
inspections to identify unattended unlocked workstations.

## 4.0 Exceptions

Business or technical exceptions must be documented, risk-assessed,
approved by an authorized owner, and reviewed at a defined interval.

## 5.0 Enforcement

Violations may result in security coaching, retraining, remediation
tickets, or progressive administrative action in accordance with
applicable organizational policy.

## 6.0 Review

This policy should be reviewed at least annually and after material
changes to the organization's endpoint, identity, or regulatory
environment.
